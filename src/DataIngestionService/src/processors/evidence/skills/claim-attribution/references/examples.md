# Attribution examples

- Source: “Priya testified that the manager told the crew to leave.” Candidate:
  “The manager told the crew to leave.” The claimant for this reported occurrence
  is Priya; retain her reporting clause in the supporting quote.
- Source: “The letter from Mateo states, ‘I believe the invoice is incorrect.’”
  Candidate: “The invoice is incorrect.” Mateo makes the assertion. The clerk
  who filed the letter is not its claimant.
- Source: “Examiner: Dr. Hale. The parent reported poor sleep. I recommend a
  follow-up assessment.” The parent's symptom report and the examiner's
  recommendation have different claimants.
- Source: “A witness said the supervisor gave an instruction.” A personal name
  cannot be inferred. Preserve a specific source role when the schema allows it;
  otherwise retain uncertainty. A list of attendees does not identify the witness.
