# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, harassment-free, and
welcoming to everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

Expected behavior: be considerate, accept constructive feedback, focus on what
is best for the project and the people it serves, and respect others' privacy.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or issues and may block contributors
who violate this code. To report a concern, use GitHub's private reporting
feature for this repository or email the organization owner at
info@daatphoenix.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to individuals representing the
project in public.
